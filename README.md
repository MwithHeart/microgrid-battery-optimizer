# Microgrid Battery Optimizer
An energy management system that fetches solar and weather forecasts via Open-Meteo across five Nigerian cities, estimates photovoltaic (PV) power generation, and outputs the automated charge/discharge decisions for local battery storage.


## 🎯 Project Goal
The objective is to optimize solar self-consumption and improve power reliability by scheduling battery charge and discharge cycles ahead of changing weather conditions.


## 🌍 Target Locations
Forecasts and solar simulations are modeled across five distinct Nigerian climate zones:
* **Lagos** Southwest with hot, humid tropical savanna and wet equatorial climate 
* **Abuja** North central with tropical savanna climate
* **Kano** Northwest with hot tropical savanna climate with a short rainy season 
* **Port Harcourt** South-south with tropical monsoon climate 
* **Maiduguri** Far Northeast with hot, semi-arid Sahel climate


## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Language:** Python
* **Weather Data:** [Open-Meteo API](https://open-meteo.com)
